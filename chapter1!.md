# Chapter 1!

In a lush valley surrounded by towering mountains, there lived a highland cow named Coo. His fur was long and shaggy, golden and brown, floating like a cloud in the gentle breeze. Coo roamed freely with his friends, but he always felt a part of the world that eluded him: technology. Coo had a dream of joining the Highland Tech Academy, a quirky school where animals learned all sorts of subjects, from programming to digital art.

Each day, Coo would gaze longingly at a group of students gathered in a clearing, their devices clicking and clacking furiously as they learned the wonders of coding. He often overheard conversations about "git," a magic tool that helped them keep track of their projects. But what did it all mean? He was determined to find out.
